## Products

These are the controllers that we generally recommend for people who are looking to purchase a drum. None of these are affiliated with the guide and are solely endorsed by our personal experience using them.

### ZhongTaiko ($549-$1089)

[taiko.ac](https://taiko.ac)

Generally considered the best controller for arcade replica. Supports all platforms and English customer support.

### TaikoForce Lv6 (¥2095/$466)

[Taobao (China)](https://world.taobao.com/item/wap/560834032433.htm) / [Yuancon Store (International)](https://yuancon.store/controller/taiko)

Highly regarded controller and was the standard for many years. Supports consoles and PC. Limited international customer support.

### Rhythmagica TDC10 ($145)

[Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/Rythmagica-Controller-Consoles-Switch-Sensitivity-Nintendo/dp/B0D62WX6HW)

Without a doubt the best controller for players on a budget. Tatacon size, portable, and supports rolling. Supports all platforms including mobile.
